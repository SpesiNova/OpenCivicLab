# Project Governance

This repository is an open discussion and review project—not a political party or a completed constitutional blueprint.

## How changes are proposed

1. Open an Issue describing the proposed change, the affected articles, and the rationale.
2. Document objections, counter-evidence, and viable alternatives. Criticism is welcome.
3. Submit textual amendments as Pull Requests with a clear and reviewable diff.
4. Participation in this project does **not** grant political authority or legitimacy. Any real-world constitutional transition would require its own democratically legitimate process.
5. Unresolved questions about fundamental rights, fairness, or discrimination must be clearly marked as unresolved.

## Transparency

Moderation decisions should be accompanied by reasons. Contributions are assessed on substance, not political affiliation or educational credentials. No project maintainer has the authority to declare this draft binding law.
