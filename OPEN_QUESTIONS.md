# Known Risks and Unresolved Questions

The questions below are **not** problems already solved by this proposal.

| Risk | Question for public review |
| --- | --- |
| Manipulated eligibility pool | Who defines suitability, and who oversees those definitions? |
| Psychological suitability | Which methods are reliable, fair, lawful, and compatible with fundamental rights? Absolute certainty is impossible. |
| Cognitive thresholds | How can relevant competence be assessed without unjustified social bias? |
| Knowledge-gated voting | Does it conflict with political equality? What workable alternatives exist? |
| Selection by lot | Who verifies the randomness and integrity of the underlying eligible population? |
| Captured oversight bodies | How can gradual capture of all oversight institutions be prevented? |
| AI veto | How can model reliability, conflicts of interest, and shared failure modes be tested? |
| Compulsory civic service | What limits are imposed by liberty, family life, employment, and human rights? |
| Emergency powers | How can exaggerated threats and artificially prolonged emergencies be detected? |
| Constitutional transition | How could a fundamental change of state institutions be democratically legitimised? |
| Diversity of opinion | How can minorities remain protected against even an expert-backed majority? |

**Review rule:** For every proposed safeguard, investigate whether the safeguard itself opens a new path for attack.
