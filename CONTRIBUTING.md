# Contributing

OpenCivicLab is an open forum for constructive disagreement. You do not need a political affiliation or academic credentials to participate.

## Contributions we welcome

1. **Adversarial stress tests:** Describe a realistic way an actor could manipulate the proposed institutions.
2. **Evidence:** Link relevant research, historical cases, or well-documented practical experience.
3. **Alternative text:** Propose a concrete, testable amendment to a specific article.
4. **Impact assessments:** Identify unintended consequences and people who may be affected.

## Standards for discussion

- Criticise ideas and arguments, not other people's dignity.
- No threats, glorification of violence, discrimination, personal attacks, harassment, or disclosure of private information.
- Separate verifiable facts from uncertainty, predictions, and personal values.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest where appropriate.
- No fabricated supporters, forged research, or coordinated deceptive campaigns.
- Contributions may be moderated. Where practicable, moderation decisions should be reasoned and open to review.

**Note:** How this repository is managed and moderated is itself part of the governance experiment and must remain open to criticism.
